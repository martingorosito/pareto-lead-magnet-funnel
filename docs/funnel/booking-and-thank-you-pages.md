## Build it in GoHighLevel (about 30 minutes)

1. **Create the calendar.** Calendars → Calendar Settings → New Calendar → Personal Booking (or Round Robin if Pareto's team takes the calls). Name it `Right Hand Fit Call`.
2. **Set the details:** 30-minute duration, 15-minute buffer, US business hours (for example 9 am to 5 pm Eastern, Monday to Friday), minimum notice 4 hours, visitor's time zone shown, video link via Google Meet or Zoom.
3. **Booking form fields:** first name, email (prefilled from the survey because the contact already exists), plus one optional question: "Anything you want us to look at first?"
4. **Confirmation:** set to redirect to `/call-confirmed` or show the message above. The booking workflow sends the confirmation email and the reminder.
5. **Build the page.** In the funnel, open the `/book` step, build the 9 sections, and add a Calendar element in section 3 pointing at the calendar.
6. **Book a test slot** from a private window as your qualified test contact. Check the slot is in the right time zone, the contact moves to Call Booked, and the confirmation email arrives.

**Button:** Start my audit now → links to the interactive audit page `/audit`. Deliver immediately: the best thank-you pages do.

## 2 · Next steps (Day 8 part 2)

**Heading:** What to do in the next 10 minutes

1. **Open the audit** (here or from your inbox). If it isn't there in two minutes, check Promotions or Spam and drag it to your inbox.
2. **Block 10 minutes today** and fill it in on screen or on paper.
3. **Write down two numbers:** your Bottleneck Score and your Hand-off Hours. We'll follow up by email with ideas on what to do with them.

## 3 · A quick win while you're here

**Heading:** Start with the system that frees the most hours

For most founders, that's the inbox and calendar. Try this today: create one rule that moves every newsletter and notification out of your main inbox, and share your calendar with one person on your team so they can book meetings for you. Small, but you'll feel it tomorrow.

**More free reading from Pareto:** The Bottleneck · How to Delegate as a Founder · The Second Brain for Founders → paretotalent.com/guides

## 4 · Objection handling (Day 8 part 4)

**Heading:** "I'm not ready for help yet." That's fine.

- **"My business isn't big enough yet."** The audit works at any size. Run it every quarter: the day your Hand-off Hours pass 10 is usually the day a Right Hand pays for itself.
- **"I tried delegating and it didn't stick."** Most founders hand off tasks, not systems. The last page of the audit shows you how to hand off a whole system in 30 days, with a recorded walkthrough and a daily end-of-day report.
- **"I don't have time to train someone."** That's why the 30-day plan starts with recording yourself once, not training anyone. Five minutes of video is your first SOP.

**Quote:** *"It's only day 2 and I'm already feeling such a relief."* · Daneen Goncalves
