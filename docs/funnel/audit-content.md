## Page 2 · Before you start

**Heading:** "I'm the bottleneck in my own company."

**Body:** That's the sentence we hear most from founders. Not because they're bad at delegating, but because nobody ever showed them **what to delegate first**. So they delegate nothing, and every decision, reply and follow-up waits for them. In 10 minutes you'll turn "I'm drowning" into a ranked list with hours on it.

### Warm-up: which of these are true this month?

Pareto analysed 1,500+ calls with founders. These six signals came up again and again. Tick every one that's true for you.

| ☐ | I have no time left for strategy, family or my own health. | 70% |
| ☐ | I've been burned by a VA or assistant before. | 60% |

**Signals ticked: ___ / 6** · Ticked 3 or more? Keep going: the next page shows where the hours hide.

### How to score (read once, takes 20 seconds)

Every system on the next page is work a trained, trusted person can own. For each one, write two numbers:

- **A. Who runs it?**
  - **0** = someone else owns it end to end
  - **1** = someone else does it, you check or approve
  - **2** = you do most of it, with a little help
  - **3** = only you. If you stop, it stops.
- **B. Hours a week.** Your honest best guess of the hours you personally spend on it each week. Round to the nearest hour. A guess is fine: precision doesn't change the answer.

**Tip:** if you're torn between two scores, pick the higher one. Founders almost always underestimate this.

## Why 8 systems and not 14

The first draft scored all 14 systems from the Day 3 stat, with three columns each: 42 answers over 7 pages. That breaks the promise of "under 10 minutes". This version merges them into the 8 where founders lose the most hours, and drops the "does it truly need you?" column by listing only work a trained person can own. The result is 16 answers over 5 pages, with the same score bands (rescaled from 0-42 to 0-24), Hand-off Hours, cost formula and call to action.
