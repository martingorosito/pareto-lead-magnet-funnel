## How the plan was built

We planned backwards from go-live.

1. Before launch, every asset link has to be live and tested in a logged-out window. So the launch brief and the leadership walkthrough are done by the evening of Oct 6.
2. The walkthrough shows a working funnel, so the funnel, automation and ads have to be finished by the morning of Oct 6. The automation needs a working form, so the funnel pages go live by the end of Oct 4.
3. The funnel copy depends on the offer, the title and the qualification criteria, so strategy is locked on Oct 3.
4. Each day ends with a milestone. If one slips, the buffer is the morning of Oct 7, never the QA block.

Most of the time goes to the funnel, automation and creative (Oct 4 to Oct 6), because that's where the leads come from.
