# The lead magnet and the offer

Why this lead magnet, who it is for, how founders are qualified, and what the next step is.

**The three goals of the launch, and how this offer serves each:**
- *Position Pareto as the authority on delegation and founder freedom:* the audit is a delegation diagnostic built on Pareto's own frameworks (14 systems, the 80% rule from Pareto's guides).
- *Grow the list with a lead magnet founders genuinely want:* it's useful even if they never talk to Pareto. They leave with a ranked hand-off list and a 30-day plan.
- *Move qualified founders into a call about the Right Hand Program:* the form routes founders who meet all five criteria straight to the booking page.

---

## The format: a self-scoring PDF audit (checklist plus scorecard)

**What it is:** a short PDF the founder fills in on screen or on paper in under 10 minutes. They tick which of the 14 business systems still run through them, estimate hours per week on each, total a score, and get a ranked "hand off first" list and a 30-day handoff plan.

**Why this format:**
- **It's the fastest format to build well before the October 7 launch.** A Google Doc or Canva PDF, shared publicly from Drive, is live the moment it's exported. No app to debug, so the remaining days go into the funnel, automation and ads, which are worth 15 of the 25 points.
- **It still does the job of a quiz.** Competitor research showed a plain PDF qualifies no one (BELAY) while a quiz qualifies well (Get Staffed Up). The audit keeps the PDF's speed and the quiz's diagnosis: the founder scores themselves, and the qualifying questions live on the opt-in form, where GoHighLevel can route them.
- **It produces a number.** Like Time etc's calculator, it ends with hours per week the founder could hand off. A number is the strongest pull toward a call.
- **Upgrade path if time allows:** the same questions can become a Lovable mini app later. The content doesn't change.

---

## The promise: one specific problem

> In under 10 minutes, find the 10+ hours a week you're spending on work someone else should own, and know exactly what to hand off first.

The single problem: **the founder doesn't know what to delegate first, so they delegate nothing.** That's the root of "I'm the bottleneck in my own company". The audit turns a vague feeling into a ranked list with hours on it.

Why "10+ hours a week": it lines up with Pareto's Freedom 40 Guarantee (40+ hours reclaimed in 30 days, about 10 a week), so the lead magnet's promise and the Right Hand Program's guarantee say the same thing.

## The title (MAGIC formula)

**Recommended title:**

> **The Founder's Bottleneck Audit: Find the 10 Hours a Week You Should Never Touch Again**

The "under 10 minutes" promise lives in the body copy and the subtitle (for example: *Takes under 10 minutes. Ends with your hand-off list.*), so the title stays focused on the outcome.

| MAGIC | In the title | Why |
|---|---|---|
| **M**agnet | "Bottleneck" | It's the client's own word ("I'm the bottleneck in my own company", Day 8). It hooks because founders already say it to themselves. |
| **A**vatar | "The Founder's" | Calls out the reader immediately. Not "business owners" or "leaders". |
| **G**oal | "Find the 10 hours a week you should never touch again" | The outcome, not the process. |
| **I**nterval | (in the subtitle and body copy) | "Under 10 minutes" sits right under the title instead of in it, so the title leads with the outcome. "A week" in the goal also frames the result in time. Hormozi says to use 3 to 5 components; this title uses 4. |
| **C**ontainer | "Audit" | Names the vehicle and implies a result at the end. |

Alternatives if you want a shorter cover line:
- *Get Your Week Back: The Bottleneck Audit for $500K+ Founders* (adds a revenue qualifier to the avatar)
- *The 14-System Bottleneck Audit for Founders*

### How the title pushes the Value Equation

Value = (Dream Outcome × Perceived Likelihood) ÷ (Time Delay × Effort and Sacrifice)

- **Dream Outcome, up:** "10 hours a week you should never touch again" is specific and permanent, not "save time".
- **Perceived Likelihood, up:** "Audit" implies a method with a score, not a motivational read. Inside, Pareto's real numbers (100+ founders served, 93% still together at 12 months, 4.9/5 across 84 Google reviews) back it up.
- **Time Delay, down:** the result arrives in the same 10 minutes, not after weeks.
- **Effort and Sacrifice, down:** tick boxes and add up a score. No setup, no hiring decision, nothing to install.

## Qualification criteria: who counts as qualified

A lead is **qualified** only if all five are true. Each maps to one form question.

| # | Criterion | Form question (answer that qualifies) | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Founder or decision maker** | "What's your role?" → Founder / Co-founder / CEO / Owner | Pareto's ICP is founder-led companies and the founder signs. A manager researching for someone else isn't ready for a call. |
| 2 | **Revenue of $500K a year or more** | "Annual revenue?" → $500K to $1M, $1M to $5M, $5M+ | Ivan's stated ICP floor (Day 3). Below it, $3,600 a month plus a $3,000 placement fee is too large a share of revenue. |
| 3 | **Business based in or serving the US** | "Where is your business based?" → United States | Pareto matches Right Hands with US founders and works in US time zones. |
| 4 | **Ready to invest in full-time help** | "A full-time Right Hand starts at $3,600/month. Is that within your budget?" → Yes, or Yes if the fit is right | Saves a call that ends at the price. Pareto only does full time, no part time (Day 1). |
| 5 | **Wants help within 3 months** | "When do you want help in place?" → Now / Within 30 days / 1 to 3 months | Pareto's sales cycle is about two weeks (Day 3). Later than 3 months is nurture. |

**Why ask this up front:** in their "We Hit $1M Gross in 1 Year" Q&A (May 2025), Ivan went through Pareto's 7 cancellations. Most came from a mismatch or the wrong client: a founder with no real business, two with unrealistic expectations, one who wanted "grunt work", one who didn't know what to delegate. Kasim's rule is "only serve your actual client, not everyone who has money." The five questions screen for exactly those cases before a call is offered.

Everyone else is **not qualified**: they land on the thank-you page, get the audit by email and enter the 3-email nurture, which re-invites them to book if their situation changes.

Two questions that **don't qualify but do segment** (used for tags and email personalisation):
- "Have you worked with a VA or assistant before?" → Yes, it went well / Yes, it didn't work out / No (feeds Persona 2 messaging)
- "Where does most of your week go?" → Inbox and calendar / Clients and delivery / Admin and finance / Sales and follow-ups (feeds the nurture angle)

**Why $500K and not the website's "$100K+/mo":** Pareto's matching page speaks to "7 & 8-figure founders" doing $100K+ a month. I set the floor at $500K a year because that's the ICP floor Ivan gave in class (Day 3), and the audit is meant to widen the top of the funnel. A founder at $500K who can invest $3,600 a month is still a fit, so Pareto gets more qualified calls without booking founders who can't afford it.

**The offer on the booking page:** a free 30-minute **Right Hand Fit Call**. You bring your audit, Pareto's team reviews your top hand-off list with you, and you leave with your first 30 days of delegation mapped, whether or not you hire.

- **Likelihood:** 100+ founders served, 93% still together at 12 months, 1 in 1,000 applicants accepted, 4.9/5 rating. Real testimonials from Justin Donald, Joe Polish and Jon Vroman.
- **Time delay:** three matched profiles within 24 hours of the matching call, and a structured 30-day integration with a Success Manager.
- **Effort and risk:** three guarantees take the risk off the founder:
  - **Matching Guarantee:** no payment until you're excited about your match.
  - **Freedom 40 Guarantee:** 40+ hours reclaimed in 30 days, or the next month is free.
  - **Lifetime Replacement:** if the fit isn't right, Pareto replaces them, any time.
- **Value stack beyond the person:** weekly Delegation Mastermind with 100+ founders, a Second Brain build-out, and ongoing training for the Right Hand.

**One line for the booking page:** *You found the 10 hours. Now meet the person who takes them, matched to you in 24 hours and guaranteed.*

**How this closes the loop the guidelines ask for:** the audit makes the founder feel the bottleneck in hours; the call shows that a vetted, guaranteed Right Hand is the fastest way out. Conclusion: *I need a Right Hand, and Pareto has one for me.*
