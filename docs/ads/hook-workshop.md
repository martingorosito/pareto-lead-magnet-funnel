# Ad hooks, more dramatic

Each option is two lines: white first line, emerald second line, as on the current ads. ★ = my pick for that ad.

How these were written:
- **Wish, then twist.** Line 1 is what the founder hoped for. Line 2 is what they got.
- **Under about 12 words.** People read on-image text in about a second while scrolling.
- **Specific images, not adjectives.** "Switchboard" works harder than "overwhelmed".
- **Meta rules respected.** No claims about the reader's health, money or feelings as facts ("you're exhausted", "you're broke"). Meta can reject ads for those.

---

## Ad 1 · The Founder With No Life
Current: *You built a company for freedom. / It gave you a job.*

- ★ **You built a company hoping for freedom. / It hired you as its only employee.**
- You built it chasing freedom. / It handed you a second shift.
- You built it searching for freedom. / It locked you in. *(closest to your partner's example)*
- Freedom was the plan. / Now the business won't let you leave.

## Ad 2 · The Bottleneck Agency Owner
Current: *You didn't start an agency / to forward emails.*

- ★ **You dreamed of leading a team. / You became its switchboard.**
- You built an agency to do great work. / Now you just pass the messages.
- Every client. Every question. Every "quick one." / All of it runs through you.

## Ad 3 · The Burned-Before Coach
Current: *After the third assistant, / you start to think it's you.*

- ★ **Three assistants. Three goodbyes. / You started blaming yourself.**
- You trusted them with your voice. / They gave it back as noise.
- After the third assistant, / you stopped handing anything off.

## Ad 4 · The Dropped-Ball Founder
Current: *You're not disorganized. / You're the only system.*

- ★ **Every invoice. Every follow-up. Every promise. / All of it lives in your head.**
- You're not forgetting things. / You're carrying too many.
- Your business has one system. / It's you, and it's full.

## Ad 5 · The Established Firm
Current: *Offshore hiring is a gamble. / Unless someone else carries the risk.*

- ★ **You've built something worth protecting. / Don't gamble it on a stranger.**
- One wrong hire can undo a year of work. / Unless someone else carries the risk.
- Offshore hiring is a gamble. / We hold the risk. You keep the hours.

---

**A caution on going further.** Lists like "searching, willing, hoping, longing" read well spoken aloud, but on an image they slow the scroll and push the message to the end. One strong verb ("hoping", "chasing") gets the drama without losing the reader. The longer, more poetic versions suit the primary text above the image, where people read more slowly.
